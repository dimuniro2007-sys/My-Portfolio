About Me

Who is
Dimuthu Danansuriya?


I'm Dimuthu Danansuriya, a multidisciplinary animator and artist from Sri Lanka who is passionate towards making art that combines inventive storytelling with technical precision.

My journey started with traditional art at the age of about 4 - 5. I was fascincated by the art created by humans over centuries. As I started developing a hobby for art, I drew everyday as it calmed me and spoke to me in an emotional way. as time skipped forwards, my hobby had become my passion.

My passion for traditional art further developed into a much broder spectrum where I could see myself appreciating all types of art media.

As I have developed a lot of creative strength and skill, I'm currently open for freelance commissions, studio collaborations and creative partnerships globally.

WHAT I BRING

3D is my primary medium and one of 2 disciplines I've invested the most amount of time into. I work specifically in Blender across full concepts to final renders.

When animating, I use a lot of references and often record myself doing a movement and match that movement with the 3D character in the scene. This is a technique most professional animators use to accurately depict human and animal behaviour.
